Desktop Pet Robot

Bluetooth control · Reactions · Eye tracking · Clock · Calendar

Not connected

Open this page from HTTPS in Chrome on Android. Turn on the ESP32; the picker filters for DesktopPet only. Do not pair from Android Bluetooth Settings.

Main controls

Press and hold for about 650 ms to open a screen. A short tap changes the pet's reaction.

Look pad

Touch and drag: the pet's eyes follow your finger. Let go and they return to normal.

Reactions

Quick actions

Normal mode shows the pet only. Clock and calendar appear only when requested.
