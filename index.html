Face tracking

Place the phone next to the pet with the front camera facing you. The pet's eyes follow your face and it greets you when you sit down.

Draw on the pet

Robot Wi-Fi

Connect Bluetooth to see the robot's Wi-Fi status.

The password is sent over Bluetooth and saved inside the robot. The ESP32 works only with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. No phone handy? Tap Hotspot, join "DesktopPet-Setup" and the setup page opens.

Notifications

Double-tap Left opens the notification centre on the pet. A web page cannot read your Android notifications. Mirroring WhatsApp, calls and SMS needs a small Android companion app, which can be added next. Until then you can send alerts from here.

Settings

The time is synced automatically every time you connect, so the clock stays correct even without the RTC battery.
